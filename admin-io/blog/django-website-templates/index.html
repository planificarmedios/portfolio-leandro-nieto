2. Argon (Free)

💎 Free

Why we like it: Bootstrap 4-based Django template with 100 elements, regular updates, and comprehensive documentation for beginners and experts.

You probably already noticed a pattern in this list of the best Django website templates: a mix of premium and free options.

Argon is a free alternative that follows the latest trends and uses only modern technologies. It’s based around Bootstrap 4, guaranteeing exceptional performance across all modern devices and platforms.

Aside from a collection of one hundred (yes, that’s 100!) elements, Argon also includes regular updates. This one is more premium than free.

3. Material (Free)

💎 Free

Why we like it: Material-Design-inspired Django template with 60 editable components, 5 color filters, and Docker deployment scripts.

Here you go, Material free. A simple-to-use Django website template that you can activate to set up a quick prototype. Note that Material is no random free template since it has sixty editable components. You even get five preset color filters!

Material works for management systems, CRMs, admins and the like. The Material-Design-inspired look also ensures the atmosphere of your new dashboard will be top-notch.

Moreover, Material also contains deployment scrips for Docker and Gunicorn/Nginx stack with other useful stuff that will do you well.

Why develop your next application from scratch if you don’t have to? You don’t even have to pay for it.

Choosing the Right Django Template

When selecting a Django template, consider your project’s specific requirements, Python expertise level, and long-term maintenance needs. The free templates are perfect for learning Django and building prototypes, while premium templates offer production-ready features, professional support, and regular updates aligned with Django’s release cycle.

All premium templates listed include lifetime updates, comprehensive documentation, and are built following Django best practices, ensuring your application remains maintainable and scalable.
